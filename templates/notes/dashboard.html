Trending and leaderboards

Score = recency × (0.7 × weighted rating + 0.3 × normalised downloads)

  • Weighted rating = (v / (v + m)) × R + (m / (v + m)) × C, where R is the note's average rating, v its number of ratings, C the average of all ratings ({{ ranking.global_mean|floatformat:2 }} right now) and m = {{ ranking.min_votes|floatformat:"-1" }}. A note with few ratings is pulled towards C, so a single 5-star vote can't top the chart. Unrated notes score 0 for this part.
  • Normalised downloads are scaled 0 to 5 against the most-downloaded note.
  • Recency = 0.5age / half-life{% if ranking.half_life_days %}: with a half-life of {{ ranking.half_life_days|floatformat:"-1" }} days, a note's score halves every {{ ranking.half_life_days|floatformat:"-1" }} days.{% else %}: decay is switched off in this deployment.{% endif %}
